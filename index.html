✳ House rules

Selfish by design, generous by accident.

Three rules. Laminated. Stuck to the fridge. Occasionally followed.

😤
01

Annoyance-driven development

No market research. No personas. Just me, muttering "there has to be a better way" at midnight, then building it.

🐜
02

Ant-sized on purpose

Each app does one thing and refuses to do eleven others. If you want bloat, there are a thousand VC-funded options.

🤝
03

No funny business

No ads, no tracking, no "sign up with 14 social accounts." Your data stays yours. I barely want my own data.

✳✦◍✳

Found a bug?
That's a feature request.

Poke around, break things, then tell me about it. I read every email, usually while nodding sympathetically.
